# Usability test plan with elderly users

Code can't answer whether the app works for the people it's for. Run this with 5 to 8 older adults (ideally 65+,
mixed comfort with phones, some with reading glasses or a tremor) and 2 to 3 family caretakers, on the device they
would really use (usually a phone). About 40 minutes each. Don't help unless they're stuck for more than a minute.

## Tasks (elder)
1. Sign in with the access code on a printed card. (Note: typing errors, use of the eye icon, time taken.)
2. Find which medicine is due next and mark it as taken.
3. You tapped "Taken" on the wrong medicine. Fix it.
4. Tell the app how you're feeling today.
5. Find when your next doctor's appointment is.
6. Have the app read today's medicines aloud. Switch the language to Hindi and back.
7. You feel unwell and want your son to call. What do you do? Then: it was a mistake; tell them.
8. Sign in again using "Email me a code".

## Tasks (caretaker)
1. Add a new medicine from a prescription photo, including dose and times. Did the review step catch anything?
2. Approve a medicine suggested by another family member.
3. Your mother forgot her code. Give her a new one.
4. Check whether reminders are being sent, and what an email looks like.

## What to record
- Completion (yes / with help / no) and time per task.
- Mis-taps: buttons pressed by accident, especially Taken / Skip / I need help.
- Reading problems: text size, contrast, words they didn't understand (note the exact words).
- Whether they understood "Not confirmed yet" versus "Not taken".
- System Usability Scale (10 questions) at the end, read aloud if needed.

## Things this round should settle
- Is large text (on by default for the elder) large enough? Are buttons big and far enough apart?
- Is a 12-character code workable on a card, or do most people prefer "Email me a code"?
- Does a single tap on "I need help" cause false alarms, and is "It was a mistake" found when needed?
- Are the Hindi strings natural to native speakers? (They need a native speaker's review before release.)
